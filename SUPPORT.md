# Customer Support Operating Procedure

Standard operating procedures for managing customer inquiries, bug reports, and refund requests for PyLaunchpad.

---

## 1. Support Channels

Customers receive developer-level assistance through:
- **Public Bug Tracker:** GitHub Issues on `lorthris/pylaunchpad` (for reproducible defects and documentation clarifications).
- **Direct Email Support:** Dedicated developer contact channel provided in Polar.sh order receipts.

---

## 2. Response SLAs

- **Initial Acknowledgement:** Within 24 business hours.
- **Defect Resolution / Patch:** Within 48 hours for critical regressions; next scheduled minor release for non-blocking enhancements.

---

## 3. Support Scope

### In Scope
- Assistance with environment setup, dependency installation, and running local tests.
- Troubleshooting Polar.sh webhook verification and checkout configurations.
- Resolving verified defects within the provided codebase.
- Guidance on deploying the provided Docker Compose and Caddy stack.

### Out of Scope
- Bespoke feature engineering or custom product design.
- General Python or FastAPI programming tutorials unrelated to PyLaunchpad architecture.
- Third-party hosting provider outage diagnosis.

---

## 4. Refund Policy

PyLaunchpad offers a **14-day money-back guarantee**:
If a customer encounters a technical defect in the codebase that prevents normal operation as described in the documentation, and our team cannot resolve it within 5 business days of notification, a full refund will be processed through Polar.sh back to the customer's original payment method.
